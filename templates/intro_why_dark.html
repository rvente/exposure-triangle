Why this site is dark

A short note about the screen you're using.

Lightroom, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, the photographer's darkroom — all dark for the same reason: your own iris. A dark UI lets your pupil open wider so your eyes pick up subtle differences in tone. Same reason movie theaters are black.

Your eyes are doing photography on this page too.

A few of the renders ahead — particularly the iris bokeh — are encoded for HDR and reach well past your screen's normal SDR ceiling. Set your display brightness to its maximum so those highlights actually shine.
