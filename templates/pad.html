How this works

Live viewfinder renders at a fixed 1/120 s so iris and ISO feel instant — you see the depth of field and noise change in real time.

Snap freezes time and integrates over your chosen capture duration with samples jittered across both the aperture disk (for bokeh) and the shutter window (for light trails). The sample budget scales with shutter duration — a long exposure takes real time to develop, which is the simulation of taking the picture.
